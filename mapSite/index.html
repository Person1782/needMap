How NeedMap makes this recommendation
  1. Reports in the same category are grouped by a named administrative polygon, not a fixed radius.
  2. Region size, place type, available population density, and rurality determine the geographic context.
  3. Urgency is weighted locally: low = 1, medium = 2, high = 3.
  4. A weighted geographic median proposes the candidate point and is constrained to the verified region shape.
  5. Gemini receives the verified region facts plus grouped category, count, urgency, and summary text.

It is decision support, not a final site selection. A human must confirm feasibility.

Sources used

  • Community reports in the Firebase incomingSignals collection.
  • Firebase AI Logic and Gemini text generation.
  • Leaflet with OpenStreetMap map tiles.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim for cached region names, boundaries, and available population tags, following its usage policy.
