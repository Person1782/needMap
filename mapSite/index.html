How recommendations and estimates work

Reports in the same category and named region form a service candidate when at least two reports are available. Urgency weights of 1, 2, and 3 help place a candidate within that region. AI can organize the supplied evidence; people still need to check the site, demand, and feasibility.

Cost and impact estimates use the assumptions shown in each plan. Report counts are not population counts. Travel comparisons are scenarios, not measured routes or guaranteed outcomes.

Map and region data: OpenStreetMap contributors. Analysis: Firebase AI Logic.
